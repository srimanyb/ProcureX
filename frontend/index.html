Addressing Problem Statement SIH26032

The Problem We Solve

Traditional procurement creates uncertainty and hardship for Indian farmers. ProcureX replaces queues with smart digital scheduling.

⏳

Long Waiting Times

Farmers often spend unnecessary days and nights waiting in queue outside mandis, leading to transport costs and produce deterioration.

📅

Lack of Schedule Information

Without advance slot booking or capacity awareness, farmers arrive without knowing when their crop will actually be inspected and weighed.

❓

Procurement Uncertainty

Farmers face zero transparency regarding quality approvals, weight slips, and Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) payment timelines.
